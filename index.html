Features

Everything for playing on several accounts

Built for people who play with alts, trade, or just want their friends' accounts in one place.

Tabs & split screen

Every account gets its own tab. Show one game, two side by side, or a grid of four – keyboard and mouse go where you click.

Party join

Send a whole category of accounts into the same server with one click – or let each join on its own.

Server browser

Pick servers by players and ping, join a friend's account's server, or paste private-server links.

Saves your PC

Hidden tabs drop to power-saving mode and can be muted. A live panel shows CPU, RAM and GPU per game.

Safe by design

Logins are encrypted with your Windows account. Lock Lumora with Windows Hello or a PIN, and back everything up with your own password.

Make it yours

12 themes, your own colors, corner radius and fonts, plus snow, stars, aurora, embers and more in the background.

Roblox Home

Open the Roblox app itself as any account to change avatars or chat with friends – without logging in and out.

Screenshots & stats

Capture any game with one click, browse your gallery, and see how long you played on every account.

Auto-reconnect

Disconnected? Lumora rejoins the same server automatically – and can restore your last session on start.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Lumora free?

Yes. Lumora is free to download and use.

Is it safe? What happens to my login?

You sign in on Roblox's own login page. Lumora only stores the resulting session on your PC, encrypted with your Windows account – it is never sent to anyone else. You can additionally lock Lumora with Windows Hello or a PIN.

Is this a cheat or an exploit?

No. Lumora doesn't modify Roblox, inject anything into games or automate gameplay. It starts the normal Roblox client for each account and arranges the windows.

Is this allowed by Roblox?

Lumora is an independent tool and not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation. Running several Roblox clients isn't an officially supported feature, so use it at your own discretion and always follow Roblox's Terms of Use.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) with the Roblox app installed. Mac and mobile are not supported.

How many accounts can I run?

As many as your PC can handle. Tabs you aren't looking at use much less CPU and memory thanks to power-saving mode.

How do updates work?

When a new version is available, Lumora tells you at start and installs it with one click.

Windows says "unknown publisher" – why?

Lumora is a young project and isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". Only download Lumora from this page.
